He ran toward the gunfire.

Nearly one year ago, on the first night of Chanukah, during the Bondi Beach massacre, Leibel Lazaroff was shot twice after running into the line of fire to save lives.

Following 11 surgeries, just hours after being released from the hospital he made his first public appearance at Sydney Opera House as he performed piano for thousands of people during the memorial service for those murdered.

Now, he's coming to Jacksonville, to the UNF Robinson Theater to share his story of survival, faith, and Jewish pride.
